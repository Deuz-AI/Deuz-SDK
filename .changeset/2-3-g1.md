'@deuz-sdk/react': minor

`useChat` now handles approvals and client tools the same way on every path. An approval verdict counts only when it answers the gate the chat is paused on: its `approvalId` must be in `pendingApprovals`, and a `token` on the verdict must be that request's token. Anything else is a no-op that sends nothing and changes no state. Before, a verdict with nothing pending, a double click, a card left over from an earlier turn or a replaced transcript, or another run's verdict sent a new POST and added an empty assistant bubble, or rode along in the next gate's `approvalResponses`. A repeated `tool-approval-request` frame is now one pending approval. A turn read back through `reconnect()` or `resume.auto` is now handled like one read from the POST: its unresolved client tool calls reach `onToolCall` (and the chat continues with a POST) or park in `pendingToolCalls`. Before, they reached neither. A replayed call runs at most once per mounted hook. It is skipped when its result is already in the replay, when the canonical history already answers it, or while it is parked or running. Across a reload the hook can only know a call ran if the restored `history.canonical` holds its result. A `stop()` during a resume read no longer appends the stopped turn, and the next `reconnect()` continues that turn from the cursor. A reconnect after a dropped POST stream rebuilds the turn from the cursor instead of folding the replay into the partial turn a second time. There are no API or type changes. The frozen `@deuz-sdk/core/react` hook is unchanged.
