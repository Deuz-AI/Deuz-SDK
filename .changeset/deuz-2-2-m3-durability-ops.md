'@deuz-sdk/core': minor
---

Durable runs can move between processes. A new `@deuz-sdk/core/ops` entry adds leases (`createInMemoryLeaseProvider`), and `@deuz-sdk/core/ops/sqlite` / `@deuz-sdk/core/ops/postgres` add durable lease providers and native agent run stores. Swarms take a `lease` option: the executor renews its run's lease on a heartbeat, a second executor gets `SwarmLeaseError` `'held'`, and one that loses its lease stops writing (`'lost'`). New operations: `handle.drain()` (settles `suspended` with a `run.drained` event), `swarm.recover()` (takes over running runs without a live executor, using the new `listRuns` store capability `'list'`), `swarm.requestCancel(key)` (`'signalled'`, `'recorded'` or `'settled'`), and `expectedRevision` / `expectedStatus` on resume. `@deuz-sdk/core/swarm/postgres` adds a Postgres swarm store whose every write is one revision-gated statement. `AgentRunEnvelope.revision` increases on every save and stores reject a stale one; `createInMemoryAgentRunStore` now enforces it too. A queued lease `'cancel'` stays queued across a change of hands and a release until a holder receives it, so a signalled cancel survives a holder that crashes or settles first, and the next executor applies it before dispatching anything. `recover()` resolves to `{ handles, failed }`, reports a run it cannot resume instead of rejecting, pages through up to 10 000 running runs with the new `listRuns` `after` cursor, and a zombie executor whose write meets the revision check rejects with `SwarmLeaseError` `'lost'`.
